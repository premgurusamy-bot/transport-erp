# Driver guide (Android app / driver web app)

**Safety first: park safely before using the app. Never use the phone while driving.**
பாதுகாப்பாக நிறுத்திய பின் மட்டுமே பயன்படுத்தவும். ஓட்டும்போது கைபேசியை பயன்படுத்தாதீர்கள்.

## Sign in
Open **GRL Driver**, enter the server address given by the office (first time only) and your mobile number → **Send OTP** → enter the OTP.
Attached/temporary drivers can sign in only while a trip is assigned; access ends automatically when the trip is completed or cancelled.

## Before the trip
1. Open the trip → **Accept trip & allow trip GPS**. Your location is shared only during this authorised trip.
2. **Pre-trip inspection** – mark each item OK or Defect. A defect in brakes, steering, tyres, lights etc. stops the trip until the vehicle is repaired and released.
3. At pickup tap **Arrived at pickup**. Scan (or type) the label on **every package** while loading. A package from another trip is flagged immediately.
4. The office authorises dispatch after loading, documents and checks are complete.

## On the road
One-tap buttons: **Departed**, **Arrived at drop**, **Delay +1h**, **Start/End rest**, **Breakdown**, **SOS** (red).
SOS alerts operations at once with your location. In an accident, call **112** first for police/ambulance.

## Delivery
Tap **Deliver** for the consignment → **Send OTP to consignee** → enter the OTP the receiver gives you, packages delivered, receiver name,
condition → **Submit POD**. If anything is short or damaged, record it honestly – the office reviews it; you are not blamed automatically.

## Expenses
Enter toll/parking/hamali etc. with a photo of the bill. Accounts reviews every expense. Your cash expenses are repaid in your settlement.

## No network?
Keep working. Events, scans, expenses and locations are saved on the phone and sent automatically (or with the sync button) when the
network returns. They keep their original time. Delivery OTP needs network; without it use signature/photo POD.

## Earnings and help
**My earnings** shows each trip's calculation (rate × quantity) and whether it is pending approval, approved or paid.
No deduction is made without approval. If something is wrong, tap **Raise grievance** – a person reviews it.
